STREET WARRIORS BY SILVERBIRD (1989)
====================================
Original game: Delvin Sorrell

The crazy hack
--------------
STREETWARRIORS+28D crazy trainer hack is the standard game of Street Warriors by Silverbird but with additional 28 in-game features that have been added by Hackersoft. Press the following keys during game play to turn on/off the extra features:

   1 = Infinite Lives On
   2 = Infinite Lives Off
   3 = Freeze Energy
   4 = Unfreeze Energy
   5 = Freeze Timer
   6 = Unfreeze Timer
   7 = Freeze Baddies
   8 = Unfreeze Baddies
   9 = Infinite Credits On
   0 = Infinite Credits Off
   + = Speed Walking
   - = Normal Walking
   £ = Get $99999
   W = Border Colour
   E = Screen Colour
   R = Scenery Colour 1
   T = Scenery Colour 2
   Y = Sprite Colour 1
   U = Sprite Colour 2
   I = Suit Colour 
   O = Teleport Left*1
   P = Teleport Centre*1
   @ = Teleport Right*1
   A = Alien Palent Mars!
   S = Snowy Day!
   D = Darkness!
  <- = Level Skip
  C= = Game Ending!
 
Notes
-----
*1 Use this feature if ever you get cornered by the baddies! 

Remember - the original game is always there and you don't have to press anything if you don't want any of the features - which is a little different than the usual trainers that only give you options before it decrunches and then you are stuck with your decisions. More accessibility for the player and much more fun overall :)


The game
--------
The Dragon Gang led by the infamous "Boss" are on your patch. They are raiding the shops and terrorising the neighbourhood.

Your task is to rid the streets of the Dragon Gang. Every time you kill a member of the Dragon Gang he will drop his wallet, which you will need to recover.

When you have cleared the area you are in, you will need to find the loot bag that the Dragon Gang members were carrying. This will allow you to enter another area.

Before entering a new area you can use the loot you have captured to purchase time, energy, your bomb, credits etc. from the local stores to help you in your fight.

Eventually you will meet "Boss". To kill him you will need to use your bomb that you should have purchased.


Controls

Joystick in port 2.
