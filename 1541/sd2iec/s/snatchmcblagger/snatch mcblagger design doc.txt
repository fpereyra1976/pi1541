SNATCH McBLAGGER
--------------------------
THE STORY:
Scotland, 1962...
During an unscheduled tea break, the guards at Fharthmoor Prison allow semi-notorious cat burglar Snatch McBlagger to escape. 
And so McBlagger begins an epic crime spree that would have made a smashing film...

INSTRUCTIONS:
Guide McBlagger out of jail and into various locations to steal valuable loot.
Joystick/keyboard controls:
LEFT/RIGHT= Walk.
FIRE= Toss a sausage laced with sleeping drug. Helpful against vicious dogs (but not much else).
UP/DOWN= Climb.
DOWN+LEFT/RIGHT= Crawl. Also a great way to dodge unfriendly fire (the only kind of fire in this game).

You get an extra life for every 10,000 points you score.

COPPERS are Snatch McBlagger's main problem. 
If they spot him (a copper's line of sight is marked with a moving "?"), he will get caught.
If they catch him, he will get clobbered.
DOGS will attack McBlagger, unless he can avoid them... or feed them until they fall asleep.
SLEEPWALKERS wake up and make a fuss if McBlagger runs into them.
SNAKES are sheer poison. Avoid them at all costs.
SPOTLIGHTS are searching for McBlagger. If he gets caught in one, it's bye-bye.
PRISONERS are not going to help McBlagger escape! (He owes them money.)

ITEMS:
- Sausage: 100 points
- Flashlight: 100 points
- Money bag: 1,000 points
- Gold: 2,000 points
- Painting: 2,000 points

HINTS & TIPS:
- Explore the environment. There are lots of hidden rewards to find.
- Different paths will lead to different results. (NOTE: Some of them must be found...)
- Play cleverly to collect the maximum number of items.
- Timing is essential. You must be quick to hide, but also quick to move on.
- Some coppers are short-sighted, but will search for McBlagger more thoroughly.
- Listen to sound cues. They may alert you to immediate danger.
- Grey dogs are faster, hungrier and more vicious.
